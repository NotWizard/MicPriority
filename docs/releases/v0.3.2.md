插着接收器但发射器已经关闭时，这一路输入不再占着你的麦克风位置。

## 🎉 新增功能

- 支持 Hollyland / MOMA（猛玛）LARK 系列 USB 接收器：发射器全部关闭时跳过该输入，后备麦克风自动接替；发射器重新开机约 2 秒后切回。
- 可用性来自接收器自身的只读状态查询，不会修改任何设备设置；静音和说话停顿都不会触发切换。

## ⚠️ 注意

- 目前完成真机验证的是 HOLLYLAND 接收器 `3547:0411`。其他型号或固件若不回应状态查询，仍按系统状态判断，不会被屏蔽。
- DJI 与小米遥控器（SayAll）的判断方式保持不变。

## English

A receiver left plugged in no longer occupies your input while its transmitters are off.

## 🎉 New

- Hollyland / MOMA (LARK series) USB receivers are supported: when every transmitter is off the receiver is skipped and the next microphone takes over; your preferred input returns about 2 seconds after a transmitter reconnects.
- Availability comes from a read-only status query to the receiver itself. No device settings are changed, and mute or pauses in speech never trigger a switch.

## ⚠️ Notes

- Physical verification currently covers the HOLLYLAND receiver `3547:0411`. Other models or firmware that never answer the query keep the system's own judgement instead of being hidden.
- DJI and Xiaomi remote (SayAll) behaviour is unchanged.
